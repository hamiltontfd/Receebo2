Receiptr – Product Requirements Document

Product Overview

Receiptr is a responsive web application that allows users to store, organize, search, and retrieve purchase receipts and invoices from both physical and online stores.

The product uses OCR and AI to automatically extract information from uploaded receipts, making them instantly searchable and easy to retrieve whenever proof of purchase is needed.

Core promise:

Never lose a receipt again.

⸻

Problem

Consumers regularly receive receipts and invoices for purchases but struggle to keep them organized.

Receipts are often:

* Lost in drawers
* Forgotten in photo galleries
* Buried in email inboxes
* Difficult to find when needed for returns, exchanges, warranties, or proof of purchase

The challenge is not storing receipts. The challenge is finding them later.

⸻

Product Goal

Enable users to:

1. Upload a receipt in seconds.
2. Automatically extract important information.
3. Search naturally for any purchase.
4. Retrieve the original receipt instantly.

⸻

Target Audience

Primary Audience:

* Consumers who purchase electronics
* Consumers who purchase home appliances
* Homeowners
* Families
* People who value organization

Secondary Audience:

* Freelancers
* Self-employed professionals
* Small business owners

⸻

Core User Story

As a consumer, I want to store receipts in a searchable digital archive so I can instantly find proof of purchase whenever I need it.

⸻

MVP Features

Authentication

Users must be able to:

* Create an account
* Log in securely
* Log out
* Recover access if needed

⸻

Receipt Upload

Users must be able to:

* Take a photo using their device camera
* Upload an image
* Upload a PDF

Supported formats:

* JPG
* PNG
* PDF

The original document must always be preserved.

⸻

OCR Processing

After upload, the system should extract:

* Store name
* Purchase date
* Total amount
* Full receipt text

The extracted information should be stored automatically.

⸻

AI Extraction

The system should analyze receipt content and identify:

* Merchant
* Purchase date
* Total amount
* Purchased products when available

Example:

Receipt:

“LG OLED C4 65”

Extracted Product:

“LG OLED C4 65 Television”

⸻

Receipt Library

Users should have access to a personal library containing all uploaded receipts.

Each receipt card should display:

* Merchant name
* Purchase date
* Total amount
* Receipt preview

Default sorting:

* Most recent first

⸻

Intelligent Search

Search is the primary feature of the application.

Users should be able to search using:

Product Names

Examples:

* OLED
* LG TV
* PlayStation
* Refrigerator

Store Names

Examples:

* Worten
* IKEA
* Amazon

Dates

Examples:

* June 2026
* Last year

Amounts

Examples:

* 1299
* 500€

Natural Language Queries

Examples:

* TV I bought at Worten
* LG products
* Expensive electronics
* Purchases from Amazon

Search should work across:

* Structured data
* Product names
* Full OCR text
* AI-generated metadata

⸻

Receipt Details

Users should be able to open any receipt and view:

* Original image or PDF
* Merchant
* Date
* Amount
* Identified products
* Full OCR transcription
* AI-generated summary

Example summary:

“Purchased an LG OLED C4 television from Worten on June 1, 2026 for €1,299.”

⸻

User Experience Principles

Fast Capture

Uploading a receipt should take less than 30 seconds.

Search First

The primary purpose of the product is retrieval.

Search should always be prominent and accessible.

Minimal User Input

Users should rarely need to manually enter information.

Automation should handle most of the work.

Trust

Users must feel confident that their receipts are securely stored and easy to recover.

⸻

Pricing

Subscription only.

No permanent free plan.

Free Trial

7-day free trial.

Paid Plan

€1/month

Includes:

* Unlimited receipts
* Unlimited uploads
* Unlimited searches
* OCR processing
* AI extraction
* Secure storage

Users may cancel at any time.

⸻

Success Metrics

The MVP is considered successful if:

* Users can upload a receipt in under 30 seconds.
* Users can locate a receipt in under 10 seconds.
* OCR successfully extracts key information from the majority of receipts.
* Users perceive Receiptr as more reliable than storing receipt photos in their phone gallery.

⸻

Out of Scope for Version 1

Do not build:

* Warranty tracking
* Warranty expiration alerts
* Asset management
* Home inventory management
* Family sharing
* Accounting tools
* Expense reporting
* Tax management
* Cashback features
* Loyalty programs
* Native mobile applications
* Email receipt forwarding

Version 1 should focus exclusively on storing, organizing, searching, and retrieving receipts.
